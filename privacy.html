Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

Who uses this app

Personal mail assistant is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not available to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app has read-only access to the owner's own Gmail account. It cannot send, delete or change email.

How the data is used

The app reads the owner's messages to summarise them, sort them and prepare draft replies for the owner to review. This runs on the owner's own computer or server. Message content is also sent to the AI model provider the owner uses (Anthropic) so that it can produce those summaries and drafts.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the model provider named above.

Storage and removal

The sign-in and any notes the app keeps are stored on the owner's own machines. The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can go to the owner through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
